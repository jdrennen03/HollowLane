Evidence

✓ the ghost leaves it  ·  ✕ confirmed it doesn't. Two evidences are never enough on their own: every pair is shared by at least two ghosts.
Behaviour. Every ghost has a tell you can spot with no equipment at all; it is written on its card. In Nightmare one of its three evidences never appears, so watch and listen.
EMF reader. Ghost interactions leave readings of 2 to 4. Only some ghosts push it to level 5.
Spirit box. Turn it on in the dark near the ghost, then ask a question. Some ghosts answer.
UV light. Sweep walls, doors and light switches the ghost touched for glowing handprints.
Laser projector. Place it in the ghost room and switch it on. Watch the grid for a silhouette.
Thermometer. The ghost room is always colder. Some ghosts drop it to 0 °C or below.
Loose objects. Anything the ghost can throw, you can carry in your free hand: E picks it up, G drops it, left click throws it.
Incense bundle. Not evidence, a lifeline. Press R to light it; it burns for about ten seconds. If the ghost comes near the smoke during a hunt it flees for a few seconds and can't touch anyone. Lit near a calm ghost, it delays the next hunt.
Camcorder. Switch it on (R) and look through it: night vision. In the ghost's room, some ghosts show as floating orbs only the camera can see.
Spirit journal. Set it down (G) near where you think the ghost is and check back. Some ghosts write in it.
Sanity medication. Four cans on the tent table. Drink one (R) for +40% sanity. One can each, so share them out.
Sanity. Darkness and ghost sightings drain it. Under 50% the ghost can start hunting (a Demon can hunt at any sanity). Run, break line of sight, or hide in a closet and close the door.
